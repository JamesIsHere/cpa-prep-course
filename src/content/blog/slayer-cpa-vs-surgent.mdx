# Slayer CPA vs Surgent: Which Is Better?

Surgent is one of the more popular mid-tier CPA review courses. It's known for its adaptive learning technology (A.S.A.P. Technology) that adjusts study plans based on your performance. But at $1,599–$2,999, it's still a major investment.

Slayer CPA offers a different model: straightforward self-study tools at $29.99/month. Here's how the two compare across the areas that actually matter.

## Pricing

This is where the two courses diverge the most.

**Surgent** offers three tiers: Essentials ($1,599), Premier ($2,499), and Ultimate Pass ($2,999). The Ultimate Pass includes "until you pass" access, but the lower tiers have limited access windows. Surgent frequently runs sales that discount these prices by 30–40%.

**Slayer CPA** costs $29.99/month with no contract. Cancel anytime. A full 12-month subscription runs about $360 — roughly 75–88% less than Surgent even at sale prices.

| Course     | Price              | Access Period  |
|------------|--------------------|----------------|
| Surgent    | $1,599–$2,999      | Varies by tier |
| Slayer CPA | $29.99/month       | Month-to-month |

For candidates paying out of pocket, the monthly model eliminates financial risk. If you pass in 6 months, you've spent $180 total.

## Study Approach

**Surgent** is built around adaptive learning. Their A.S.A.P. Technology assesses your knowledge through a diagnostic exam, then creates a customized study plan that focuses on your weak areas. The system adjusts as you progress, spending less time on topics you've mastered.

**Slayer CPA** is self-directed. You choose which sections and topics to study, in whatever order you want. Lessons are text-based with worked examples, followed by quizzes with detailed explanations. There's no algorithm deciding what you study next — you control the sequence.

If you want software to manage your study plan, Surgent does that well. If you prefer to own your study strategy and move through material at your own pace, Slayer CPA gives you that flexibility.

## Content Format

**Surgent** uses a mix of video lectures, textbook-style readings, and practice questions. Videos are shorter and more focused than Becker's — typically 10–30 minutes. They also include audio lectures you can listen to on the go.

**Slayer CPA** is entirely text-based. Each lesson covers a topic with explanations, examples, key formulas, and practice problems. After studying, you take a section quiz. There are also timed practice exams and downloadable PDF study frameworks (concept maps, decision trees, formula sheets).

No video lectures in Slayer CPA. If video is essential to your learning style, Surgent has an advantage there.

## Question Banks

**Surgent** has over 8,000 practice questions with detailed explanations. Questions are integrated into the adaptive system, so you'll see more questions in areas where you're struggling.

**Slayer CPA** has 4,400+ questions across all six CPA sections. Questions are organized by topic with full explanations for every answer choice. Practice exams simulate the real exam format with timing and question navigation.

Surgent has a larger raw question count, but Slayer CPA's 4,400+ questions cover every AICPA Blueprint topic area. For most candidates, question quality and explanation depth matter more than raw volume.

## Section Coverage

Both courses cover all six CPA exam sections: AUD, FAR, REG, BAR, ISC, and TCP.

**Surgent** aligns content to the AICPA Blueprint and updates regularly. Their editorial team has been maintaining CPA content for years.

**Slayer CPA** has 96 lessons mapped to the 2026 AICPA Blueprint with current IRS figures and tax provisions. Each section includes topic-specific study frameworks.

## Pass Guarantee

**Surgent** offers a pass guarantee on their Ultimate Pass tier ($2,999). If you don't pass, you get continued access until you do. The lower tiers don't include this.

**Slayer CPA** has no pass guarantee, but at $29.99/month, your financial exposure is minimal. If you need an extra month, it costs $30 — not $2,999 upfront for a guarantee you might never use.

## Who Each Course Is Best For

**Choose Surgent if:**
- You want adaptive learning to manage your study plan
- You learn well from short video lectures
- Your employer is covering the cost
- You want a pass guarantee and are willing to pay for the top tier

**Choose Slayer CPA if:**
- You're paying out of pocket and want minimal financial risk
- You prefer self-directed study over algorithm-driven plans
- You like text-based learning with detailed explanations
- You want downloadable study frameworks for offline review
- You want month-to-month flexibility with no long-term commitment

## The Bottom Line

Surgent is a solid mid-tier option with strong adaptive technology. If you're the kind of studier who benefits from software guiding your study plan and you have the budget, it delivers.

Slayer CPA is built for candidates who want effective study materials without the $1,500–$3,000 price tag. At $29.99/month, you get 96 lessons, 4,400+ questions, practice exams, and study frameworks — the core tools you need to pass.

Both courses cover the same exam. The question is whether adaptive technology and video lectures are worth $1,200–$2,600 more to you. If you're a disciplined self-studier, Slayer CPA gives you everything you need at a fraction of the cost.
