# CPA Question Style Guide

Quality rubric for all CPA exam practice questions. Every question in the question bank must meet these standards before deployment.

## Stem Rules

### Scenario-First for L2+ Questions

Application, analysis, and evaluation questions must lead with a scenario:

**Pattern:** `[Named Entity] [did action in context]. [Question about consequence/treatment/requirement]?`

```
Good: "Harper & Associates CPA firm is auditing Ridgemont Manufacturing's
December 2025 financial statements. During fieldwork, the audit team discovers
that management made an accounting estimate for warranty reserves using assumptions
that differ from historical experience. Which AU-C section addresses the auditor's
responsibilities for evaluating accounting estimates?"

Bad: "Which auditing standard addresses accounting estimates?"
```

### Named Entities Required

Use realistic fictional names, not generic placeholders:

```
Good: "Oakridge Construction LLC"
Bad:  "the entity", "Company A", "the client"
```

### Specific Dollar Amounts and Dates

Calculation questions must include concrete numbers:

```
Good: "On March 15, 2026, Thornton Industries purchased equipment for $185,000..."
Bad:  "A company purchased equipment..."
```

### Word Count Targets

| Difficulty | Target Words | Pure "What is X?" Allowed? |
|------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Easy       | 10-20       | Yes, up to 30% of topic   |
| Medium     | 20-40       | No                        |
| Hard       | 30-60       | No                        |

### L1 Cap

Maximum 30% of questions in any single topic can use pure definitional "What is X?" / "Which describes X?" stems.

## Distractor Rules

### Every Wrong Answer Must Reflect a Real Error

Each incorrect choice should represent a specific misconception, common computational error, or misapplication of a rule that students actually make:

```
Good distractors for "What is the correct basis?":
- $85,000 (forgot to add closing costs)
- $110,000 (included seller's property taxes)
- $95,000 (used fair market value instead of cost)

Bad distractors:
- "Basis cannot be determined"
- "None of the above"
- An obviously wrong number with no pedagogical value
```

### Parallel Grammatical Structure

All four choices must follow the same grammatical pattern:

```
Good:
A) Debit Accounts Receivable $50,000
B) Credit Sales Revenue $50,000
C) Debit Cash $50,000
D) Credit Accounts Receivable $50,000

Bad:
A) Debit Accounts Receivable
B) You would credit Sales Revenue for the full amount
C) Cash should be debited
D) $50,000 to AR credit
```

### Length Parity

The longest choice must be no more than 2x the length of the shortest choice. The correct answer must not be systematically longer than distractors.

### Banned Patterns

These patterns are prohibited in all questions:

| Pattern                        | Reason                                       |
|--------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| "All of the above"            | Tests test-taking skill, not knowledge        |
| "None of the above"           | Tests test-taking skill, not knowledge        |
| "Both A and C"                | Creates dependency between choices            |
| "absolute assurance"          | Lazy wrong-answer trap (CPA exam never uses)  |
| "guarantee" as distractor     | Same as above                                 |
| "ensures with certainty"      | Same as above                                 |

## Explanation Rules

### Required Structure

Every explanation must follow this three-part structure:

1. **Sentence 1:** State the correct answer and cite the controlling standard
2. **Sentence 2:** Explain WHY the correct answer is right (the reasoning)
3. **Sentence 3:** Explain why the most plausible wrong answer is wrong

### Word Count Target

50-100 words per explanation.

### Standard Citations Required

Always name specific standards by section number:

```
Good: "Under AU-C 315, the auditor is required to obtain an understanding of
internal control relevant to the audit."

Bad: "Under auditing standards, the auditor must understand internal controls."
```

### Common Citation Formats

| Section | Standard Format                                   |
|---------|--------------------------------------------------|
| AUD     | AU-C 200, AU-C 315, SQMS No. 1, AT-C 105       |
| FAR     | ASC 606, ASC 842, ASC 820, GASB 34              |
| REG     | IRC Section 1031, Circular 230, UCC Article 2   |
| BAR     | ASC 815, ASC 805, GASB 34, ASC 718              |
| ISC     | NIST CSF, COBIT, SOC 2 TSC, AICPA TSP 100      |
| TCP     | IRC Section 409A, IRC Section 2503, IRC 704(d)  |

### Address Wrong Answers

Use contrast language to explain why the most plausible distractor is wrong:

```
Good: "While choice B (modified opinion) might seem appropriate, AU-C 705 requires
a qualified opinion only when misstatements are material but not pervasive."

Bad: "The other answers are incorrect."
```

## Difficulty Definitions

### Easy

Single rule, direct application. One concept, no complications.

**AUD example:** "Under SQMS No. 1, which of the following is a required component of a firm's system of quality management?"

**FAR example:** "Under ASC 606, revenue is recognized when control of a good or service transfers to the customer. Which of the following indicates control has transferred?"

**REG example:** "Under IRC Section 1031, which type of property qualifies for like-kind exchange treatment?"

### Medium

Rule + complicating factor. Requires applying a rule in context with an exception, threshold, timing issue, or interaction.

**AUD example:** "Westfield Industries has $2.3 million in total assets and $450,000 in net income. The auditor set overall materiality at $50,000 and performance materiality at $37,500. During testing, the auditor found $42,000 in aggregate uncorrected misstatements. What is the auditor's most appropriate response?"

**FAR example:** "On January 1, 2026, Evergreen Corp. enters a 5-year lease for office space with annual payments of $48,000 due at the beginning of each year. The implicit rate is 6%. The right-of-use asset should be recorded at what amount?"

**REG example:** "Marcus purchased rental property in 2020 for $275,000 (excluding land). He used straight-line depreciation over 27.5 years. In 2026, he sells the property for $310,000. The adjusted basis at sale is $225,000. How much of the gain is subject to unrecaptured Section 1250 gain at 25%?"

### Hard

Judgment between two plausible positions, or multiple interacting rules. Requires evaluating competing considerations.

**AUD example:** "During the audit of Pacific Ridge Medical Group, the audit team discovers that management's going concern assessment relies on a projected contract renewal that has not yet been finalized. Management asserts the entity will continue as a going concern based on historical renewal patterns and preliminary verbal commitments. The auditor has obtained the draft contract but notes the terms differ materially from the existing agreement. Under AU-C 570, which response is most appropriate?"

**FAR example:** "Cascade Holdings owns 35% of Summit Corp. and exercises significant influence. Summit reports net income of $2,400,000 and declares dividends of $800,000. However, Summit also reports an unrealized loss on available-for-sale securities of $300,000 in OCI. Under the equity method, what amount should Cascade report as investment income, and how should the OCI component be treated?"

**REG example:** "A married couple files jointly with AGI of $385,000. They have a $45,000 passive loss from a rental activity in which the spouse materially participates as a real estate professional, a $12,000 passive loss from a limited partnership, and $8,000 of net investment income. Determine the deductible passive losses and the net investment income tax liability."

## Bloom's Cognitive Level Definitions

### Level 1: Remembering and Understanding (R&U)

Recall facts, definitions, standards. Recognize concepts.

**Stem patterns:** "What is...?", "Which describes...?", "Which statement is true about...?"

| Section | Example |
|---------|---------|
| AUD     | "Which of the following is a component of the COSO Internal Control framework?" |
| FAR     | "Under ASC 842, a lease is classified as a finance lease if it meets any of which criteria?" |
| REG     | "Under Circular 230, which of the following constitutes disreputable conduct?" |
| BAR     | "The DuPont formula decomposes return on equity into which three components?" |
| ISC     | "Which NIST Cybersecurity Framework function focuses on developing safeguards to ensure delivery of critical services?" |
| TCP     | "Under IRC Section 469, which of the following is classified as a passive activity?" |

### Level 2: Application

Apply rules to specific scenarios. Calculate amounts. Determine correct treatment.

**Stem patterns:** "Calculate...", "Determine the amount...", "What is the balance after...?", "[Entity] [action]. What is the correct treatment?"

| Section | Example |
|---------|---------|
| AUD     | "Mercer & Associates is planning the audit of Dalton Manufacturing. Total revenues are $45 million. Using the percentage-of-revenue benchmark, what is the appropriate overall materiality?" |
| FAR     | "On April 1, 2026, Brighton Corp. issues $500,000 face value 8% bonds at 103, with interest payable semiannually. What is the carrying amount of the bonds on the issue date?" |
| REG     | "Rodriguez purchased a rental duplex for $320,000 in 2022. In 2026, Rodriguez exchanges it for an apartment building valued at $410,000 plus $15,000 boot. What is Rodriguez's basis in the new property?" |
| BAR     | "Pinnacle Corp. has EBIT of $2,400,000, interest expense of $600,000, and a tax rate of 21%. Calculate the degree of financial leverage." |
| ISC     | "A SOC 2 Type II report covers the period January 1 through December 31, 2025. An exception was identified in March 2025 and remediated by April 15. The service auditor should report this exception in which section of the report?" |
| TCP     | "A client has $180,000 in passive income from a limited partnership and $95,000 in passive losses from a rental activity. The client's AGI is $165,000. How much of the rental loss is deductible?" |

### Level 3: Analysis

Evaluate the effect of one factor on another. Compare alternatives. Identify implications.

**Stem patterns:** "What is the most likely effect of X on Y?", "Which factor most likely indicates...?", "How would X affect Y?"

| Section | Example |
|---------|---------|
| AUD     | "During testing, the auditor discovers that the client's automated controls over revenue recognition were modified mid-year without documentation. What is the most likely effect on the auditor's risk assessment and planned audit procedures?" |
| FAR     | "A company changes its inventory method from LIFO to FIFO in a period of rising prices. What is the effect on the current year's cost of goods sold and ending inventory compared to the prior year?" |
| REG     | "A taxpayer converts a personal residence (basis $350,000, FMV $280,000) to rental property. Two years later, the property is sold for $310,000. Analyze the tax consequences, considering both the loss limitation on conversion and the depreciation taken." |
| BAR     | "A municipal government reports a $2.5 million general obligation bond issue. Compare the effects of reporting this bond in the government-wide statements versus the governmental fund statements." |
| ISC     | "An organization implements multi-factor authentication for remote access but continues to allow single-factor authentication for local network access. Analyze the most likely residual risk this configuration creates." |
| TCP     | "A shareholder of an S corporation has a stock basis of $50,000 and a loan basis of $20,000. The S corporation reports a $60,000 ordinary loss and a $15,000 separately stated charitable contribution. Analyze how these items flow through to the shareholder." |

### Level 4: Evaluation

Make judgments. Recommend courses of action. Assess competing positions.

**Stem patterns:** "Evaluate...", "Recommend...", "Assess whether...", "Should the auditor...?", "What is the best course of action?"

| Section | Example |
|---------|---------|
| AUD     | "The audit engagement partner disagrees with the client's revenue recognition policy for long-term construction contracts. The client's policy is within GAAP but differs from the industry norm. The disagreement could result in a material misstatement. Should the auditor issue a qualified opinion, and what factors should the partner evaluate in making this determination?" |
| TCP     | "A high-net-worth client with $12 million in assets is considering establishing either a grantor retained annuity trust (GRAT) or an irrevocable life insurance trust (ILIT) for estate planning purposes. The client is 62 years old and in good health. Evaluate which strategy better achieves the client's goal of minimizing estate taxes while maintaining some control over assets." |

## Tone Rules

Based on the best-performing questions from the question bank:

1. **Realistic fictional names** — Use proper nouns that sound like real businesses: "Ridgemont Manufacturing," "Harper & Associates CPA," "Oakridge Construction LLC"
2. **Concrete dollar amounts** — Specific numbers, not round: "$185,000" not "$200,000"
3. **Specific dates** — "March 15, 2026" not "during the current year"
4. **Explanations use "because"** — Link the rule to the conclusion: "This is correct because AU-C 315 requires..."
5. **Cite standards by name** — "Under ASC 606" not "under revenue recognition standards"
6. **Professional register** — Write at the level of a CPA exam review course, not a textbook

Before adding questions to a migration:

- [ ] Correct answer cited to specific standard
- [ ] Explanation addresses most plausible wrong answer
- [ ] Parallel grammatical structure across choices
- [ ] No banned distractor patterns
