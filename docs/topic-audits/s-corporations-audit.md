**Net bank change:** 82 → 53 questions (35% cleanup).

Drift pattern summary: unlike International Tax where the drift was dominated by named anti-deferral provisions (GILTI, Subpart F, FDII, BEAT), the S Corporations drift is concentrated in two clusters:

  (a) **Exception-tax mechanics** — BIG tax (Sec 1374), passive investment income tax (Sec 1375), the 3-year termination rule under 1362(d)(3), PTTP, the 5-year re-election bar. These are the "narrow exception webs" that the handoff doc expected would define S Corps drift; the audit confirms it, just at a lower rate than the 87% International Tax surprise.

  (b) **Topic misplacement** — loss limitation ordering (belongs in REG individual), reasonable compensation / SE tax framing (belongs in TCP owner-entity transactions), NIIT (belongs in REG individual). These are cross-topic questions that were tagged to S Corporations by mistake rather than drifted into from within.

The (a) cluster will stop recurring once new generation runs under the
reg-s-corporations topic spec constraints (commit 301dabe). The (b)
cluster is a tagging/classification issue that topic specs don't directly
prevent — it surfaces the need for a future cross-topic tagging audit as
more specs come online.
